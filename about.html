About MTVI Apparel

MTVI Apparel is a clothing brand that focuses on producing stylish, high quality clothing at an affordable price

The brand was created to give people an opportunity to expressthemselves through fashion while celebrating modern African culture

Our histroy

MTVI Apparel produces sustainably sourced, high-quality urban fashion and essentials. What started as limited-run pop-up markets has grown into a recognized local lifestyle brand operating two flagship retail spaces.
